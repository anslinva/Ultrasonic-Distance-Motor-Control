WIRING GUIDE

1. ULTRASONIC SENSOR (HC-SR04)
VCC  -  Arduino 5V
Trig -  Arduino Pin 9
Echo -  Arduino Pin 10
GND  -  Arduino GND

2. MOTOR DRIVER (L293D)
EN1 (Enable 1) -  Arduino Pin 5 (PWM Speed)
IN1            -  Arduino Pin 7 (Direction)
IN2            -  Arduino Pin 8 (Direction)
OUT1 & OUT2    -  DC Motor Terminals
VCC1 (Logic)   -  Arduino 5V
VCC2 (Power)   -  Arduino 5V
GND            -  Arduino GND (Common Ground)

3. STATUS LEDs (Connect via 220 Ohm Resistors)
Green Anode    -  Arduino Pin 2
Yellow Anode   -  Arduino Pin 3
Orange Anode   -  Arduino Pin 4
Red Anode      -  Arduino Pin 6
All Cathodes   -  Arduino GND
